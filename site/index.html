Install on Android in about a minute.

Slipbook isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install it directly. It's safe, and Android just asks you to confirm.

1

Download

Tap the button below. Your phone saves a file called Slipbook.apk.

Download Slipbook
2

Allow the install

Open the file. If Android says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings and switch on Install unknown apps for your browser (or Files), then go back and tap Install.

3

Play Protect: install anyway

If Google Play Protect warns that the app is unknown, tap More details, then Install anyway. This only appears because the app isn't from the Play Store.

Then open Slipbook, type your business name, and make your first invoice. Later updates: download the new file the same way.

Questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Making invoices, recording payments and looking at your sales all work offline. You only need data to send the PDF on WhatsApp.

Where is my data kept?

Only on your phone. There's no account and no server, so nobody else can see your records.

What if I lose my phone or reinstall?

Use Settings, Back up everything often and keep the file in Google Drive or send it to yourself on WhatsApp. On the first screen, tap Restore from a backup file. Without a backup, the records can't be recovered.

Why does my phone warn me?

Android warns about any app that doesn't come from the Play Store. Tap Install anyway.

Does it cost anything?

Version 1.6 is free.
